The ledger on this phone is encrypted with the PIN (AES-256, key derived with PBKDF2). It cannot be recovered: forgetting it means erasing the app and restoring a backup. A 4-digit PIN has only 10,000 combinations, so it slows down someone with the phone's files rather than stopping them. Backups you copy or download are not encrypted.
